I’m Nathan Mahnke, a software engineer with a Bachelor’s in Computer Science and Systems from the University of Washington. I’ve completed the Revature Pre-Training program, where I built a REST API backend using Java, SQL, RESTful APIs, and Javalin. I have one year of experience as a professional Full Stack Developer.

At RMS Corporation, I designed and implemented AI-driven automation and decision-support tools that streamlined warehouse inventory management and significantly reduced manual workload. As a consultant at M2 Advisory And B/D Consulting, I modernized client materials by integrating AI voice, redesigning workflows, and future-proofing forms for seamless database integration.

My technical toolkit includes C#, Java, Python, Spring, SQL, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, and strong expertise in databases, automation pipelines, and AI-powered solutions. I thrive on problem-solving, system optimization, and mentoring teammates. I’m passionate about creating impactful software and continuously growing as an engineer and developer.
