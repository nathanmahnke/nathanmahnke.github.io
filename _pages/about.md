I’m Nathan Mahnke, a software engineer with a Bachelor’s in Computer Science and Systems from the University of Washington. I graduated in 2023, at the age of 20. After my graduation, I started work at RMS Computer Corp. There, I worked in a small team to develop and maintain the warehouse inventory management software offered by RMS. RMS Computer Corp holds contracts with large companies across America, including Boeing. I left RMS Computer Corp after a year for M2 Advisory and B/D Consulting. I had already worked in a freelance capacity for them while still working at RMS and had a positive experience during that time. I couldn’t turn down a pay raise and a move to remote/hybrid work. I worked hybrid (in-office once a week) for the first six months, but went full remote when the owner of the company moved to the east coast. Now, the owner is looking to wind down operations and retire, so I’m looking for other employment. I currently do freelance web design for a few regular clients, but am looking for permanent and stable employment.
Throughout my short career, I have had experience with Java, C++, and Python mainly. I’ve developed full stack applications using RESTful APIs, Javalin, and SQL. I love working with small teams and strong leadership. I enjoy instant feedback and reinforcement. I work well with constructive criticism and can handle workplace disagreements without undue stress. I’ve worked in-person, hybrid, and remotely and have excelled during all three. My preference would lean towards hybrid and remote just to avoid a commute, but if I lived close to the office, I’d have no problem with in-person.
